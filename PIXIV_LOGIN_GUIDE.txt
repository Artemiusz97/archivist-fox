==============================================================================
 ARCHIVIST FOX - HOW TO LOG IN TO PIXIV (BEGINNER'S GUIDE)
==============================================================================

Pixiv requires an authorization token so Archivist Fox and gallery-dl can 
download high-resolution artwork without getting blocked.

You only need to do this ONCE. Follow these simple steps:

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: Run the Pixiv Login Shortcut
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click "pixiv_login.bat" in this folder.
(Or open a terminal here and run: npm run pixiv-login)

A black terminal window will appear and open your web browser to Pixiv.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: Open the "Developer Tools" in Your Browser
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BEFORE you click login or enter your password:

1. Press F12 on your keyboard.
   (If your keyboard has an Fn key, you may need to press Fn + F12.
    Alternatively, right-click anywhere empty on the webpage and click "Inspect").

2. A developer panel will appear on the side or bottom of your browser window.

3. Look at the top menu of this developer panel and click on the "Network" tab.
   (It will say Elements/Inspector, Console, Sources/Debugger, NETWORK, etc.)

4. [HELPFUL TIP]: Near the top of the Network tab, look for a checkbox called
   "Preserve log" (in Edge/Chrome) or "Persist Logs" (in Firefox) and check it.
   This prevents the log from clearing when the page redirects.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: Log In to Your Pixiv Account
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Now, on the main webpage:
- If you are already logged in: Click "Continue as [Your Account]" or select it.
- If prompted: Type in your email/username and password, and sign in.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: Copy the Callback Link
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After you sign in, the browser will attempt to redirect to an app link:

1. Look back at the "Network" tab that you opened in Step 2.
2. At the top of the Network tab, there is a "Filter" search box.
   Type "callback" into that box.
3. You will see an entry named "callback?state=..." or "login?code=..."
   (It might be colored red or say "canceled" - that is completely normal because
   your desktop computer doesn't have the Pixiv mobile phone app installed).
4. RIGHT-CLICK on that entry:
   - Select: Copy -> Copy URL (or "Copy link address").

(Alternatively: If your browser's address bar shows a link starting with 
 "pixiv://account/login?code=...", you can simply copy that entire address bar!)


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: Paste and Press Enter
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Switch back to the black terminal window.
2. Right-click inside the window (or press Ctrl + V) to paste what you copied.
3. Press Enter.

That's it!
You will see:
[success] Logged in as Pixiv user: ...
[success] Saved to gallery-dl cache
[success] Saved to .env (PIXIV_REFRESH_TOKEN)

Archivist Fox is now fully authorized and ready to download Pixiv links!
==============================================================================
